# Team Training Overview

This overview is for managers, partners, and reviewers evaluating the AI Security
& Governance Lab — AWS Edition as a practical training path for teams.

## The problem

AI workloads are entering AWS environments faster than many teams can review
them. Security, GRC, audit, and governance staff are asked to answer practical
questions:

- What does this AI workload do?
- Which AWS services and identities matter?
- What evidence supports the risk assessment?
- Are logging, data exposure, and guardrails reviewable?
- How should the risk be explained to leadership?

Generic AI policy training does not answer those questions. Passive cloud videos
do not prove that a learner can work a ticket, handle evidence, and communicate a
defensible recommendation.

## The offer

The lab is designed as a guided, evidence-based job simulation. Learners work
through realistic AWS AI security and governance tickets, produce sanitized
proof-pack artifacts, and practice explaining technical findings to both
technical and executive audiences.

The strongest fit is a small team cohort for:

- cloud security engineers moving into AI workload review;
- GRC and audit professionals who need practical AWS evidence literacy;
- security managers building a shared AI governance vocabulary;
- AI governance teams that need defensive, bounded, AWS-specific practice.

## What teams get

- A shared AWS AI workload scenario.
- A ticket queue that mirrors security, audit, and governance work.
- Defensive-only evidence review and analysis.
- Sanitized artifacts that can be reviewed without exposing real customer data.
- A final executive-risk narrative that tests whether learners can explain what
  matters.

## What this is not

- Not an exploit lab.
- Not jailbreak training.
- Not a compliance certification.
- Not a job guarantee.
- Not a claim of AWS partnership or official endorsement.
- Not live access to private lab materials through this public repo.
