# Responsible Use

The AI Security & Governance Lab — AWS Edition is **defensive-only**. Its purpose is to help professionals secure, audit, and govern AWS AI workloads — never to attack them.

## Principles

- **Defensive-only.** All work focuses on protection, detection, audit, and governance.
- **No exploit payloads.** The lab does not publish, request, or reward offensive payloads or jailbreak prompts.
- **No credential sharing.** Access is individual and is never shared, posted, or reused.
- **No public leakage.** Internal materials, ticket content, and evidence stay private and sanitized.
- **Synthetic by design.** The lab uses synthetic scenarios and synthetic evidence — no real customer data.

## What this work does not promise

- No guarantee of certification.
- No guarantee of employment or job placement.
- No claim of compliance certification.
- No claim of AWS partnership or official status.

The goal is demonstrable, defensible skill — shown through sanitized evidence and proof of work.
